The Pool

PGA Tour fantasy snake draft

No app store needed. Follow these four steps and The Pool lives on your home screen just like a native app.

Open this link in Safari first

bowens8.github.io/golf-pool
1

Open in Safari

Make sure you're using Safari — not Chrome, not Instagram's browser. If you tapped a link in a text message, tap the Safari icon in the bottom right corner of the screen first.

Only Safari supports "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone.

2

Tap the Share button

At the bottom of Safari, tap the Share button — it looks like a box with an arrow pointing up ⎙. If you don't see it, scroll up slightly on the page.

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down in the Share sheet until you see "Add to Home Screen" and tap it. You'll see a preview with the green icon and the name "The Pool."

4

Tap "Add"

Tap Add in the top right corner. That's it — The Pool now appears on your home screen with its own icon and opens full screen with no browser bar, just like a real app.

💡 Tips:
• Everyone in the pool should do this — it makes the experience much better than opening a browser every time.
• The app saves data to the cloud, so all five of you see the same draft and scores in real time.
• Works on iPad too using the same steps.
Not an app store app. Runs in your browser — no download, no subscription, no cost.
